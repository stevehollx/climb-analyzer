# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** December 24, 2024

**Climb Visualizer: Pocket** ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects information.

## Summary

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data remains on your device.

## Information Collection

### Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

- **Climb Data Files**: CSV/XLSX files containing road and trail climb information that you choose to download from our public GitHub repository
- **User Preferences**: Your settings such as measurement units (metric/imperial) and display preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

### Third-Party Services

The App communicates with the following third-party services:

1. **GitHub** (github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com)
   - Purpose: Download climb data files from the public repository
   - Data sent: Standard HTTP requests (no personal information)
   - Privacy Policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement

2. **Apple MapKit**
   - Purpose: Address autocomplete when searching for locations
   - Data sent: Search queries you type
   - Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3. **OpenStreetMap Overpass API** (overpass-api.de)
   - Purpose: Fetch road geometry for map visualization
   - Data sent: OpenStreetMap way IDs (public geographic identifiers)
   - Privacy Policy: https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports
- Advertising data

## Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device using iOS standard storage mechanisms. The App does not have user accounts and does not transmit your data to external servers.

You can delete all stored data at any time by:
- Deleting individual climb data files within the App
- Uninstalling the App (removes all data)

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
